# Citizenwise Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 25, 2026**

Citizenwise (“the App”) helps you study U.S. civics test content. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

## 1. Who We Are

Citizenwise is operated by the Citizenwise development team (“we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions, contact us at the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App keeps study-related information on your device so features work offline and between sessions. We do not operate a Citizenwise login or user account in the App today. Examples include:

- Study progress: streak, XP, readiness score, spaced-repetition scheduling, and internal daily learn-audio completion tracking
- Which questions you have fully heard in Learn or Practice mode (for progress and XP)
- Your selected U.S. state and congressional district (for state-specific civics answers)
- App preferences you set in Settings (appearance, study reminders, mini-game sounds, Word-Level Highlight, English Practice, and similar toggles)
- Test practice session size and other study UI choices
- A small snapshot for the optional home screen widget (see Section 6)

This on-device data is not uploaded to us by default solely because it exists in the App. It remains on your device until you delete the App or clear the App’s data using device settings (or until it is restored from an iCloud or device backup that includes app data).

## 3. Networking and Content Delivery

When you are online, the App may fetch civics questions, translation text, audio catalog metadata, and current national or state official names from our servers or third-party infrastructure (for example Supabase or similar hosting). These are read-only content requests; we do not ask you to sign in to Citizenwise to receive them.

Your device sends standard network requests. Those requests may be accompanied by technical information that web services commonly log (such as IP address, timestamps, user agent, and error diagnostics). We do not receive your study streak, XP, readiness score, or speaking transcripts through these requests.

If a request fails, the App may use bundled content stored inside the App so core study features can still work offline where possible. Audio may play from files bundled in the App or from remote storage referenced by the catalog.

## 4. Audio Playback

Question audio and Practice-mode playback occur on your device. Listening to study audio does not use your microphone.

## 5. English Speaking Practice

If you turn on English Practice in Settings and use Speak on a Practice card (civics oral practice in the App), the App uses Apple’s speech recognition and your device’s microphone to capture what you say. Processing is intended to stay on your device when on-device recognition is supported.

Spoken audio is used to compare your answer against acceptable civics responses and show feedback in the App. This is study practice only—it is not a USCIS interview or N-400 examination. We do not upload your voice recordings or transcripts to Citizenwise servers as part of this feature. Apple’s speech and microphone permission prompts explain access the first time you use Speak; you can change permissions later in iOS Settings.

## 6. Home Screen Widget

If you add the optional Citizenwise home screen widget, the App copies a small study snapshot into a shared on-device container (Apple App Group) so the widget can display it without opening the App. This snapshot may include:

- Today’s featured civics question number and prompt text
- Your readiness score percentage
- A timestamp of when the snapshot was last updated

The widget data stays on your device. It is not sent to Citizenwise servers. The App refreshes this snapshot when you open or return to the App. Tapping the widget opens the App; if a question number is included in the link, the App opens that question when your subscription tier allows access to it.

Removing the widget or deleting the App removes this shared snapshot from your device.

## 7. Study Reminders

If you enable study reminders in Settings and allow notifications for Citizenwise, the App schedules local notifications on your device. These reminders are not sent from a Citizenwise push server; they are created and stored on your device. You can turn reminders off in the App or in iOS Settings at any time.

## 8. Subscriptions and Purchases

The App offers a free tier and optional paid subscriptions through Apple’s App Store.

**Free tier (no purchase required):** questions 1–15 in English, with sample Spanish and Dari translation access on those same questions only. Practice, Test, and mini-games are available within the free question pool.

**Paid subscriptions** unlock the full 128-question USCIS civics bank in English (and, when purchased, full Spanish or Dari translation and audio for all 128 questions). Payment, renewal, cancellation, and refunds are handled by Apple under your Apple ID—we do not collect or store your payment card details.

Apple provides us with subscription entitlement status through StoreKit so the App can unlock the features you purchased. We do not receive your Apple ID password.

## 9. Analytics, Ads, and Sale of Personal Information

We do not run third-party advertising inside the App. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs to profile you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 11. Data Retention

Server-side logs and infrastructure metadata are retained according to our providers’ configurations and operational needs. On-device study data and widget snapshots are retained until you remove them by deleting the App, removing the widget, or clearing local data.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the service to protect information handled by our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 13. International Users

If you access the App from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our providers operate.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy, except where applicable law requires additional consent.
